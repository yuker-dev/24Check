Before start:
	ATIS Information acknowledged
	Flight Plan filed
	Request IFR/VFR Clearance
	
Start-up and pushback: 
	Request Push & Start
	Start the engines ("E" on PC)
	Pushback yourself ("P" on PC)
	OR 
	Wait for a pushback tug (If available)

After Start-up and Pushback:
	Engines normal (Press back "P" on PC)
	Flaps set to TO position (Flaps 1)
	
Before Taxi:
	Request Taxi Clearance

Taxi:
	Follow Taxi instructions

After Taxi:
	Request Frequency Change (With Ground only)
	Monitor/Contact Tower

Before takeoff:
	Verify Flaps position
	Verify charts (If using SID and STAR)
	Wait for Tower Clearance

Takeoff: 
	Stabilize engines at 50%
	TO/GA (100% engine thrust)

After Takeoff:
	Gear up
	Flaps Retract
	Reduce thrust to remain under 250kts
	Wait for Frequency transfer to CTR

Climb/Cruise:
	Follow ATC Instructions
	Reman at or bellow 250kts (If 3000ft and below)

Before landing:
	Set Flaps to 1 when lined up with Runway
	Set Flaps to 2-3 when Frequency change
	Reduce speed to approach speed (~ 140kts)
	Set Flaps to Full when Cleared to land 
	Reduce speed to landing speed (~ 125kts)
	
Landing:
	Flare when 30fts above ground
	Thrust Idle when 20fts above ground
	
After landing: 
	Full reverser until 70kts 
	Vacate runway
	Reset flaps positions
	Follow ATC instructions to Gate

Shutdown:
	Turn off engines ("E" on PC)
	Thank the ATC
